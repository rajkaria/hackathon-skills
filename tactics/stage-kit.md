# Demo Day Stage Kit

Physical packing list. Dongle failure 5 minutes before pitch costs more than any code bug.

**Use when:** Packing the day before demo day. Second check at T-60 min at the venue.
**Skip if:** Online-only event. (Even then, see the "remote pitch" section below.)

---

## The core packing list

### Laptop + backup
- [ ] Primary presenting laptop — charged to 100%.
- [ ] Secondary laptop with full repo + demo video loaded (can be a teammate's).
- [ ] Laptop stickers removed if they include anything off-brand or distracting.

### Power
- [ ] Primary laptop charger (spec-matched).
- [ ] Secondary laptop charger OR universal USB-C PD 65W+.
- [ ] 2-port USB-C wall adapter.
- [ ] 10,000 mAh+ power bank, charged.
- [ ] 6-outlet power strip (underrated — venues often have 1 outlet per 4 people).

### Video / display
- [ ] **USB-C to HDMI dongle** (the single most-failed item).
- [ ] **USB-C to VGA dongle** (older venues).
- [ ] **Mini-DisplayPort to HDMI** (if Mac with MDP).
- [ ] HDMI cable, 3m (don't rely on venue cables).
- [ ] HDMI-to-HDMI coupler (in case venue cable is female-female).

### Audio
- [ ] Wired lavalier mic with 3.5mm or USB-C connection (if your pitch includes a narrator).
- [ ] Headphone splitter (for on-stage demos with audio cues).

### Internet
- [ ] Mobile hotspot device OR phone with tethering confirmed.
- [ ] Secondary hotspot on a different carrier (if available).
- [ ] Ethernet dongle + 3m Ethernet cable (some venues offer wired).

### Input
- [ ] External wireless mouse with new batteries.
- [ ] External wireless keyboard (if laptop keyboard is sticky or for multi-user demo).
- [ ] Presentation remote / clicker with fresh batteries.

### Data
- [ ] USB-C flash drive with: demo video (MP4 + MOV), slide deck (PDF + PPTX), press kit folder, product logos, demo-mode database dump.
- [ ] Cloud backup verified accessible offline (not just via cloud sync — actual local copy).

### Product / brand
- [ ] 100-count QR code stickers pointing to waitlist URL + demo.
- [ ] 20 one-pager PDFs printed (handout for judges + sponsors who ask).
- [ ] 50 business cards — even in 2026, they work at physical events.
- [ ] Team t-shirts (if you have them) — visible team identity in crowd shots.

### Comfort / longevity
- [ ] Water bottle.
- [ ] Snack bar (low-sugar; avoid the crash).
- [ ] Small first-aid: ibuprofen, bandaids, eye drops, throat lozenges.
- [ ] Deodorant, toothbrush, floss. Day 3 of a hackathon is olfactory danger zone.
- [ ] Change of shirt. You will spill something.

---

## Priority bundle (if you can only bring 5 items)

1. **HDMI dongle + HDMI cable.** Single highest-impact failure prevention.
2. **Mobile hotspot.** Venue wifi will fail at least once.
3. **USB drive with offline demo video.** The final fallback.
4. **Business cards + QR stickers.** Hallway conversations happen; you must be capturable.
5. **One-pager PDFs printed.** Judges often ask for "something to take home."

---

## A/V test protocol (T-60 min at venue)

Arrive 60 min before your slot. Do not rely on venue staff's assurance.

1. **Plug into the presentation rig.** Verify signal + audio.
2. **Walk to the back of the room.** Can you read your smallest slide text?
3. **Open the live demo.** Does the venue network carry it?
4. **Run the backup MP4 on the venue laptop** if one is used.
5. **Test the mic** at expected speaking volume.
6. **Confirm the time slot.** Ask staff directly; schedules shift.

If any of those fail, trigger the mitigation from `risk-register.md` Demo day risks.

## Remote / online pitch (no physical venue)

Different kit, same discipline:

- [ ] Primary camera: external webcam at 1080p minimum. Built-in webcams look amateurish.
- [ ] **Wired ethernet connection** — do not pitch over wifi. Buy a $10 USB-to-ethernet adapter.
- [ ] Backup internet: mobile hotspot on a different carrier.
- [ ] Ring light or single key light — better to be slightly bright than under-lit.
- [ ] Wired lavalier mic or headset with boom mic — not laptop mic, not AirPods.
- [ ] Close all non-essential apps (Slack, Discord, email) to prevent notification sound leaks.
- [ ] Desktop wallpaper clean / project-themed (you may share screen).
- [ ] Screen-share rehearsal — what do judges see? Bookmark bar, tabs, desktop icons.
- [ ] Backup call-in number pasted in team chat in case video app fails.
- [ ] Zoom / Meet room opened T-10 min; teammates muted and ready.

---

## Team roles on demo day

- **Presenter** — pitches. Nothing else.
- **Driver** — advances slides, handles demo interactions. Silent unless specifically handed off to.
- **Floater** — carries backups (laptop, USB, power bank), handles unexpected venue issues, answers off-stage questions.

Establish roles at least 12 hours before demo. Switching roles day-of creates chaos.

---

## Post-pitch (the first 10 minutes after stepping off)

- [ ] Find and thank the emcee / event staff.
- [ ] Available at the booth or in the hallway — judges often follow up.
- [ ] Hand out 5-10 one-pagers to interested people.
- [ ] Don't check phone for 15 minutes — stay present.
- [ ] Reconvene with team at a pre-set spot to debrief.

---

## Before leaving the venue

- [ ] Retrieve all cables, dongles, chargers from the stage / booth.
- [ ] Confirm submission checklist (see `multi-track.md`) — did we click every "submit" button?
- [ ] Collect business cards + note names on the back before memory fades.
- [ ] Brief team photo in front of event signage (content for post-hackathon posts).
