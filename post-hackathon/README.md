# Post-Hackathon Conversion Pipeline

A submission is not the finish line. It's the starting line for a 30-day window where the prize money matters less than the follow-on capital, YC application, grants, and users you can convert out of the afterglow.

**Use when:** The moment submission closes. Not one day later.
**Skip if:** You DNF'd with nothing to show. Come back next event.

## Why this layer

The highest long-tail $ value in the entire skill lives here:

| Outcome | Probability | $ value | Effort |
|---------|-------------|---------|--------|
| Hackathon prize | ~15% (multi-track) | $5-25k | 48h intense |
| Ecosystem grant | ~40% (with templates) | $15-75k | 2-4h filing |
| YC S-batch interview | ~5% (with data room) | 7-figure opt | 3h application |
| 100 real users | ~25% (with distribution) | Company-making | 2 weeks |

One grant = more than the prize. One YC interview = more than the grant. One engaged 100-user cohort = more than YC.

## The 7-Day Fire Drill

If you do nothing else in this section, do this:

**Within 7 days of any submission (win or lose):**

1. Fire 3 grant applications using `grants/*` skeletons. Pick by ecosystem fit.
2. Publish 1 build-journey blog post. Link to repo, live demo, video.
3. Open the data-room structure (`data-room.md`). Add problem quotes + traction metrics.
4. Send 5 personal follow-ups to judges, sponsor reps, or investors who approached you.
5. Update `../career/score-ledger.json` + `../career/sponsor-crm.md`.

**Total time:** ~6 hours. **Expected return:** statistically, at least one of (grant, investor meeting, 50 waitlist signups) within 30 days.

Most teams do none of this. That's the arbitrage.

## Pre-win prep (the counter-intuitive one)

**Sign the legal hygiene doc BEFORE submission.** Reasons:
- Prize money landing into one teammate's account is a tax mess.
- IP ownership disputes kill more hackathon projects than bugs.
- A $25k win with no vesting means any teammate can walk with 33% of the company on day 2.

See `legal-hygiene.md` for the 10-line agreement that solves 95% of this. Sign it at hour -6.
