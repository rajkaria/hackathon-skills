# Coinbase Ventures — Application Skeleton

**Note:** This is **investment, not a grant.** They take equity. Typically pre-seed / seed. Pair with grants — don't replace.

**Program:** ventures.coinbase.com. Warm intros vastly outperform cold.
**Check size:** $100k-$2M typical for pre-seed/seed.
**Decision:** 2-6 weeks from first meeting.
**Best fit:** Projects with team + traction + a crypto-native distribution angle.

---

## Mental shift

Coinbase Ventures is an investor, not an ecosystem funder. They want ownership of a company that will grow 100x, not a project that will enhance their ecosystem. Pitch accordingly.

Use this template for:
- **First meeting request** (email / intro template)
- **Follow-up deck** (10-slide, same as `arsenal/pitch/variants.md` 30-minute deck)
- **Data room access** (see `../data-room.md`)

## What they look for (based on public portfolio patterns)

| Signal | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Team with crypto-native track record | Very high |
| Product already shipped with real users | Very high |
| Distribution defensibility (not just tech) | High |
| Solving a primitive other builders will depend on | High |
| Warm intro from existing portfolio / Coinbase employee | High |
| Cold application with no traction | Near-zero |

## The meeting

30 minutes. Structure:
1. 5 min — personal background, team.
2. 10 min — live product demo.
3. 10 min — thesis + market + distribution.
4. 5 min — Q&A + ask.

**Do:**
- Run the 3-min demo from `arsenal/pitch/variants.md` almost verbatim. It's refined for this format.
- Have actual metrics on a dashboard.
- Prepare the 12 questions from `arsenal/pitch/qa-combat.md`.
- Ask specifically: "What would need to be true in 6 months for Coinbase to lead a Series A?"

**Don't:**
- Open with "the market is $X trillion."
- Show whiteboard speculations about v5 features.
- Ask for feedback on the business model — ask for investment or a specific action.

## Data room must include

See `../data-room.md` for the full structure. Minimum for CBV:
- Cap table (current + post-round pro forma)
- Financial model (3-year, monthly for year 1)
- Customer letters / LOIs
- Technical architecture doc
- Team bios
- Competitive analysis
- Prior funding history
- Notable advisors / investors in current round

## Post-meeting

- Follow up within 24 hours with the artifacts they asked for. Missing this = deprioritized.
- Send a monthly update email even if they declined. Investors track teams they passed on; some invest 6 months later.

## Risks of taking this capital

- Signaling: taking Coinbase capital signals "for Coinbase ecosystem." Can help in crypto, can hurt in broader Web2-adjacent markets.
- Competitive restrictions in term sheets — read carefully.
- Board rights — pre-seed usually no board; seed might. Know what you're giving up.

**Coinbase Ventures operates like a Tier 1 VC with a crypto lens. Treat the application as a fundraise, not a grant. Warm intros, clean data room, crisp demo. The rest is timing.**
