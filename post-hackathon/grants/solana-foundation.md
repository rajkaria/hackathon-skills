# Solana Foundation Grants — Application Skeleton

**Program:** solana.org/grants. Also: Superteam Earn bounties (faster, smaller), Solana Colosseum hackathon follow-ons.
**Award:** $10-100k typical; larger for infra.
**Decision:** 3-5 weeks.
**Best fit:** Solana-native projects, novel use cases, projects pushing the chain's performance story.

---

## Framing

Solana grants reward projects that **make Solana look fast and capable in public.** Performance-visible demos score high. Replication-of-EVM-pattern projects score low.

## Parallel paths (recommended)

Apply to Solana Foundation AND post a bounty or RFP engagement via Superteam Earn. The Superteam path is faster and often funds the first-milestone gap while the Foundation application processes.

## Attachments

- Solana program IDLs or links.
- Benchmark showing a performance-specific win (e.g., "settled in 400ms, visible on demo").
- Demo video.
- Integration with a Solana-native protocol, if any (Jupiter, Kamino, Drift, Phoenix, etc).

## Post-submission

- Engage in Solana Discord / Telegram builder channels. Visibility to the ecosystem team matters.
- Log award + timeline in `../../career/sponsor-crm.md`.
- On award: tag @solana and @solanafndn in announcement; they amplify.

**Solana Foundation values: native integration, performance visibility, open source, active community engagement. Performance stories that can be demoed in under 30 seconds score highest.**
