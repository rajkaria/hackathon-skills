# Arbitrum LTIPP — Application Skeleton

**Program:** Arbitrum Long-Term Incentives Pilot Program (LTIPP successors). Check forum.arbitrum.foundation for active window.
**Award:** 50-500k ARB (distributed over milestones).
**Decision:** 4-8 weeks; community vote on STIP/LTIPP committee.
**Best fit:** DeFi infra, onboarding tools, projects that drive real TVL / user activity on Arbitrum.

---

## Key framing

Arbitrum grants reward **usage activation**, not pure research. The application asks: "given this much ARB, how much TVL / users / transactions will arrive on Arbitrum?"

Hackathon projects applying here should reposition as **distribution plays on Arbitrum**, not as general protocol development.

## Community engagement before submission

- Post a draft in Arbitrum forum `Governance` section 1-2 weeks before formal submission.
- Gather feedback from delegates. Incorporate.
- Often one or two delegates become champions who shepherd the proposal.

## Attachments

- Dune dashboard for existing usage (on any chain).
- Audit report if applicable (or scheduled audit scope).
- Integration partner logos with signed LOIs.

## Post-submission

- Attend the LTIPP committee call if there is one for your round.
- Respond to delegate questions within 24 hours on the forum.
- On award: publish the award announcement + milestone timeline within 7 days.

**LTIPP reviewers value: quantified KPIs, Arbitrum-specificity, delegate buy-in, execution track record. In that order.**
