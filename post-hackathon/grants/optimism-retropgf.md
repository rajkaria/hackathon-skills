# Optimism RetroPGF — Application Skeleton

**Program:** app.optimism.io/retropgf
**Model:** Retroactive, not prospective. Rewards *impact already delivered*.
**Award:** 50-250k OP (varies by round; sometimes higher for top projects).
**Decision:** 6-10 weeks, badgeholder voting.
**Best fit:** Shipped > 6 months, measurable on-chain impact, public good characteristic.

---

## The key mental shift

RetroPGF is NOT a hackathon grant. It rewards what you've already done. A brand-new hackathon project submitting for the current round will rank low. **Submit for the round that falls 6-12 months after the hackathon**, when you have:

- 6+ months of sustained activity.
- Measurable on-chain metrics (users, volume, integrations).
- Public-good characteristics (open source, open standards, docs, educational content).

## Badgeholder outreach (the under-used lever)

RetroPGF is voted by badgeholders. Unlike traditional grants, you can (and should) reach them.

- **Published badgeholder list** on app.optimism.io. Pull the list.
- **Match to your network** — GitHub / Twitter. Non-spammy outreach: "We submitted to RetroPGF Round X — if you have 5 minutes, here's a 1-page impact summary."
- **Community channel** — post in #retropgf-submissions in Optimism Discord with a clear summary.
- **Do not:** mass DM every badgeholder. Reputation carries.

## Attachments

- 1-page impact summary (PDF) — reviewers often only read this.
- Dune / Flipside dashboard with live metrics.
- Architecture doc.
- Link to all relevant repos.

## Post-submission

- Schedule monthly public update while voting is open — badgeholders often check recent activity.
- Voting transparency: some badgeholders post their reasoning. Monitor and respond politely if they have questions.
- Log award + round number in `../../career/sponsor-crm.md`.
- If awarded: a meaningful portion typically goes to infra + continued development, not immediate founder comp. Signals durability.

**RetroPGF reviewers value: measurable impact, OP-ecosystem specificity, open-source posture, sustained delivery. Novelty matters less than it does in prospective grants.**
