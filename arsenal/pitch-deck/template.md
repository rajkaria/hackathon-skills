# Pitch Deck Template (Markdown → Slides)

Use [Marp](https://marp.app/), [Slidev](https://sli.dev/), or [Tome](https://tome.app/) to render this into actual slides. Markdown-first means the deck stays in your repo, version-controls cleanly, and gets reviewed alongside the code.

**Slide budget for a 5-min pitch:** 8 slides. One idea per slide. No bullets. No paragraphs. Each slide should be intelligible from the back of the room.

---

## Slide 1: Hook (15 seconds)

> A specific person experiences a specific weekly pain.

**Format:** one sentence, large type, full-bleed.

> "Sarah runs a 15-person DAO. She spends 3 hours every Sunday manually sending payments to contributors."

---

## Slide 2: The Pain (30 seconds)

> Quantify. Use a real number.

**Format:** one stat, one image (Sarah's calendar / a screenshot of her process).

> 156 hours/year. $200B in DAO treasuries. No native payroll primitive.

---

## Slide 3: Solution (30 seconds)

> Your one-liner, then immediately the product.

**Format:** product screenshot or 5-second loop above the one-liner.

> **HashPay** — automated crypto payroll in one click.

---

## Slide 4: Demo (90 seconds)

> Live or pre-recorded. Show the product working. No architecture diagrams.

**Format:** screen recording auto-plays. You narrate over it.

Path through the demo:
1. Connect wallet (5s)
2. Create payroll batch (15s)
3. Fund it (10s)
4. Recipients receive — show one explorer link (15s)
5. Stats update live (5s)

---

## Slide 5: Tech & Sponsor Integrations (30 seconds)

> Quick. Specific. Cite sponsor reps by name if you talked to them.

**Format:** logo grid, one line each.

- **[Required tech]** — used as load-bearing infra (not a wrapper)
- **[Sponsor 1]** — solves [specific need] in our product
- **[Sponsor 2]** — enables [specific feature]
- **What's real:** live testnet transactions, deployed at hashpay.xyz

---

## Slide 6: Traction (15 seconds — IF you have it)

> Numbers from the hackathon week. Even small ones.

**Format:** 3 numbers, large type.

> 84 waitlist signups · 12 demo wallets connected · $4,200 testnet volume

If you don't have traction yet: skip this slide and merge with Slide 7.

---

## Slide 7: Vision (30 seconds — THE SEPARATOR)

> Where this goes in 6 months. Not "what we'd build with more time" — what the product becomes.

**Format:** roadmap with concrete dates and named users.

> Month 1: 50 DAOs onboarded
> Month 3: $1M processed on-chain
> Month 6: payroll → expense management → treasury suite

This slide is what separates a hackathon project from a company. Most teams don't have it. You will.

---

## Slide 8: Close (15 seconds)

> Memorable takeaway. Call to action.

**Format:** URL, QR code, team handles.

> **hashpay.xyz** — try it now
> @yourteam · open to grants, partnerships, beta users

---

## Speaker Notes Discipline

Write speaker notes for every slide. Practice with a timer. Most teams over-run by 30-40% on first attempt. The discipline of cutting words to fit the timer makes the pitch sharper.

A 5-minute pitch is ~700 words spoken at presenter pace. If your script is over 800 words, cut.
